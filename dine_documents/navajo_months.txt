NAVAJO MONTHS (Navajo Names for Months)

January - Yas Nilt'ees (Snow Crust)
- Named for the snow crust that forms during winter

February - Atsá Biyáázh (Eagle Baby)
- Named for the time when eagles are born

March - Wózhch'ish (Sour/Green)
- Named for the green vegetation starting to appear

April - T'ą́ą́chil (Little Leaves)
- Named for the small leaves appearing on plants

May - T'ą́ą́tsoh (Big Leaves)
- Named for the fully developed leaves

June - Ya'iishjáásh (Small Harvest)
- Named for the early harvest season

July - Ya'iishjáástsoh (Big Harvest)
- Named for the main harvest season

August - Bini'ant'ą́ą́ts'ózí (Small Ripening)
- Named for the early ripening of crops

September - Bini'ant'ą́ą́tsoh (Big Ripening)
- Named for the full ripening of crops

October - Ghąąjį' (Half Harvest)
- Named for the late harvest season

November - Níłch'its'ósí (Thin Wind)
- Named for the thin, cold winds of late autumn

December - Níłch'itsoh (Big Wind)
- Named for the strong winter winds

Notes:
- Traditional Navajo months are based on seasonal changes and natural events
- Different regions may have slight variations in month names
- These months are tied to traditional agricultural and ceremonial cycles
